# Monitoring and Alerts

## What generates a notification

Per docs/requirements.md §17, five notification types exist
(`NotificationType` enum) and all five now have something that actually
writes them:

| Type | Generated by | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| `CAMERA_OFFLINE` | `recording/health.ts`'s `checkCameraHealth()` — MediaMTX's path exists but isn't currently connected | 30 min |
| `RECORDING_STOPPED` | Same function — MediaMTX's path for a `RUNNING` job doesn't exist at all anymore (removed outside the normal stop flow) | 30 min |
| `UPLOAD_FAILURE` | `worker.ts`'s ingestion poll, when `drainPendingSegments` reports `failed > 0` for a camera | 30 min |
| `RETENTION_CLEANUP_FAILURE` | `worker.ts`'s retention job, for exactly the tenants `enforceRetention()` reports actually had a failed delete this run | 60 min |
| `STORAGE_QUOTA_WARNING` | `recording/health.ts`'s `checkStorageQuotaWarnings()` — usage at/above 80% of the plan's quota | 24 h |

## Avoiding alert storms

`lib/notifications.ts`'s `notifyOnce()` is the single chokepoint every alert
goes through: before creating a row, it checks whether a notification of
the same `(tenantId, type, targetId)` already exists within the cooldown
window, and skips if so. This was verified for real, not just in unit
logic: a camera was taken offline (stopping the `simulated-camera`
container), the health-check job (30s interval) ran multiple times while it
stayed offline, and exactly **one** `CAMERA_OFFLINE` row was created —
confirmed by querying `/notifications` after two full health-check cycles
and seeing the count stay at 1.

`targetId` is what makes the dedup scoped correctly — two different
cameras going offline at the same time produce two separate notifications
(different `targetId`), not one merged/suppressed one. A real bug was
caught and fixed while building the retention-failure hookup: the first
draft notified *every* tenant with any lingering `EXPIRED` segment whenever
*any* tenant's delete failed, which would have alerted tenants whose
retention cleanup was actually fine. Fixed by having `enforceRetention()`
return the specific `failedTenantIds` for that run, not just a count.

## What "camera offline" actually checks

`checkCameraHealth()` only looks at cameras with a `RUNNING`
`RecordingJob` — it doesn't poll every registered camera, only ones
currently being recorded. It asks MediaMTX's own runtime path status
(`GET /v3/paths/get/{name}`, `connectors/mediamtx-client.ts`'s
`getPathStatus`) rather than attempting its own RTSP probe, since MediaMTX
is already maintaining that connection and already knows its own state —
re-probing independently would be redundant and slower.

Two distinct states, deliberately handled differently:
- **Path exists but not ready** → `CAMERA_OFFLINE`. The `RecordingJob`
  stays `RUNNING` — MediaMTX retries its own RTSP pull internally and will
  resume recording on its own once the camera comes back; there's nothing
  for this app to restart.
- **Path doesn't exist at all** → `RECORDING_STOPPED`, and the
  `RecordingJob` is explicitly marked `FAILED` with a `lastError` — because
  in this case recording genuinely isn't happening, and leaving the DB
  saying `RUNNING` would be a lie.

## Verified for real (not just unit-tested)

- A healthy, actively-recording camera produces zero notifications across
  a full health-check cycle (confirmed via `/notifications`).
- Stopping the simulated camera's feed produces exactly one
  `CAMERA_OFFLINE` notification within one health-check interval, and the
  dashboard's Alerts page / nav badge reflect it.
- The cooldown holds across a second full health-check cycle — no
  duplicate.
- Restarting the feed and stopping the recording completes cleanly (the
  final drain still ingested the segment recorded before the outage, 0
  failures) — the health-check mechanism doesn't interfere with normal
  stop/drain logic.
- Mark-as-read and mark-all-as-read both work and are tenant-scoped.

## The dashboard

`apps/web/src/app/dashboard/alerts/page.tsx`: lists the 50 most recent
notifications for the active tenant, unread ones highlighted, with
mark-read/mark-all-read actions. The sidebar nav shows an unread-count
badge next to "Alerts," refreshed every 30s
(`apps/web/src/app/dashboard/layout.tsx`).

## What's not implemented

- Per-field health metrics beyond offline/stopped detection — "last
  received video frame," specifically, isn't tracked (MediaMTX's path
  status reports connected/not-connected, not a frame-level timestamp).
  `CameraConnectionSession` rows from `test-connection` partially cover
  "last successful connection" for manual tests, but aren't updated by the
  automatic health-check job.
- Delivery beyond the in-app notification list — no email/SMS/webhook
  adapter exists. `Notification` rows are the entire delivery mechanism
  today.
- An explicit "camera back online" notification — its absence is implied
  by `CAMERA_OFFLINE` simply stopping, not a separate event, since the
  schema's `NotificationType` enum (from docs/requirements.md) doesn't
  define a recovery type.
